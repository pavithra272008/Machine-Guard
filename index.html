01 / OVERVIEW

About Machine Guard

Many small workshops depend on manual inspection and may not have access to expensive industrial monitoring systems. Machine Guard explores an affordable way to collect machine-condition signals and make useful information accessible through a mobile application.

🎯 Objective

Monitor machine behaviour and identify abnormal patterns at an early stage.

🏭 Target users

Small and medium-sized enterprises, workshops and machine operators.

📱 Approach

Collect sensor readings and communicate machine status and alerts through an app.

02 / CAPABILITIES

Core features

📈 Condition monitoring

Capture vibration, motion and sound-related data to observe machine behaviour.

🔎 Abnormality identification

Examine collected readings to look for patterns that may indicate unusual operation.

🔔 Early alerts

Show warnings through a mobile application so operators can inspect the machine sooner.
